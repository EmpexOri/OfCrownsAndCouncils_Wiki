Ethiopia

Overview

Ethiopia stands as one of the oldest continuous polities in the world, shaped by imperial tradition, religious authority, and the pressures of modernity in the era of industrial empires.

Its survival against colonial encroachment and internal fragmentation defines its unique position within Africa and the wider world.

Ethiopia

Capital Addis Ababa
Government Imperial Monarchy
Primary Culture Amhara
Religion Oriental Orthodox
Founded c. 980 BC

Eras

18th Century

Ethiopia entered the century fragmented, ruled by regional princes under the nominal authority of the Solomonic dynasty.

19th Century

Centralization efforts reshaped Ethiopia as foreign pressure mounted across Africa.
